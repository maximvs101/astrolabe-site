Astrolabe — Sextant School: privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026.

Astrolabe is a free app for learning celestial navigation with a sextant, published on the App Store and Google Play by Maxime Joly, its developer and the person responsible for it (contact: mjoly.pm@gmail.com). This policy explains what the app does with your data: it collects none.

In short

  • No personal data is collected or transmitted, either to the developer or to any third party.
  • No account, no advertising, no analytics or audience measurement. The app sends no crash reports.
  • The app makes no network requests. It works entirely offline.
  • Everything you save stays on your device.

What is kept on your device

  • The sight log: the sessions you choose to save (date, dead-reckoning position, measured altitudes, latitude and longitude found, course and speed made good entered for a noon fix, notes).
  • Settings and progress: language, lighting (night or red night), chosen almanac, lessons completed.

This data is stored in a local SQLite database, in the app's private storage. It leaves the device only if you export it yourself (see below). You can delete a session from the log; uninstalling the app erases everything. Depending on your phone's settings, the system backup (iCloud, Google backup) may include this data, as it does for any app: it is then kept by your Apple or Google account, not by the developer.

Location

Your position is read only when you ask for it, on the field screens (noon sight, Polaris and noon fix), through one of two buttons:

  • "Use GPS position", to prefill your dead-reckoning position;
  • "Compare with GPS", to compare your sextant latitude with the GPS latitude afterwards or, for the noon fix, the latitude and longitude of your fix with the GPS ones (distance and direction of the gap).

The permission requested is "while using the app"; the app never accesses your location in the background. The position read is kept only in the local log, and only if you save the session (for the noon fix, latitude and longitude included). It is never transmitted. You can refuse the permission: you then enter your dead-reckoning position by hand, and the latitude calculation does not depend on it.

The position is computed by your phone's location service (iOS, or Google Play services on Android). Depending on your system settings, such as Google Location Accuracy, that service may use Wi-Fi, mobile networks and sensors, and exchange data with Apple or Google, under their responsibility and privacy policies. The app itself receives only the position and passes it on to no one.

Notifications

At your request, the app can schedule a reminder before the Sun crosses the meridian, or when the Polaris sight window opens at twilight. These are local notifications, scheduled and shown by the phone itself: no notification server is used. Permission is asked only when you schedule a reminder.

On Android, the notification module includes Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging library, intended for remote notifications. Astrolabe does not configure or use it: no identifier is created and nothing is sent.

Exporting the log

You can export your log as CSV or JSON. The file is prepared on the device and handed to the system share sheet; you choose the app or person that receives it. Nothing is sent without your action.

Links

The "About and sources" screen contains links (this policy, the sources cited, the contact address). They open in your browser or email app, only if you tap them; the policy of that browser or website then applies.

System diagnostic reports

The app itself sends no crash or diagnostic reports. If you have agreed to it in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may receive anonymous system diagnostic reports and pass some of them on to the developers of the apps concerned; this sharing is governed by their privacy policies and can be turned off in the phone's settings.

Emails

If you write to the contact address, your message and email address are used only to reply to you. The data controller under the GDPR is Maxime Joly, who processes them on the basis of a legitimate interest in replying to your request. The mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail). They are not passed on to anyone else; they are kept for the duration of the exchange, then deleted.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

The app transmits no data to the developer: the app's data is under your sole control, on your device. For an email you have sent, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and objection: just write to the contact address. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (CNIL), www.cnil.fr.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address, with its update date.
